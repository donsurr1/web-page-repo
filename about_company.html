About Us

A contract manufacturing (CM) company that manufactures electronic circuit boards for large OEMs (Original Equipment Manufaturers). The OEM design the hardware circuits, and the CMs build the boards using the designs provided. The CMs use the state-of-the-art manufacturing assembly techniques whcih include automated assembly lines where machines place the parts on pre-printed circuit boards, send them through the ovens where the parts are set ups of the placement machines for each board.
